ANSWER:
  | A departure fee of seventy-five dirhams (AED 75.00) will be collected from every passenger departing the United Arab Emirates through Dubai airports [1]. Passengers below the age of two (2) years are exempted from payment of the departure fee [1]. On-duty pilots and cabin crew are exempted from payment of the departure fee [1]. Transit passengers whose arrival and departure flight number is the same are exempted from payment of the departure fee [1]. Transfer passengers whose final destination is not the United Arab Emirates and whose departure is within twenty-four (24) hours from the time of arrival are exempted from payment of the departure fee [1]. Airlines operating in Dubai airports will be responsible for collecting the departure fee upon issuing air tickets to customers within or outside of the United Arab Emirates [2]. The fee collected pursuant to this Law will be paid to the Public Treasury of the Government of Dubai [3].

ANSWER:
  | Drivers of vehicles which are not licensed by the Licensing Authority to transport passengers must not transport them within the Emirate or from the Emirate to outside destinations [1]. A temporary authorisation to transport passengers from the Emirate to other emirates by taxicabs that are not licensed in the Emirate will be issued subject to the following conditions [2]. The temporary authorisation applicant must be an establishment licensed by the competent entity in the other emirate to conduct the activity of transporting passengers by taxicabs [2]. The cars of the temporary authorisation applicant must be licensed as taxicabs and must meet the requirements and specifications adopted in the emirate in which they are licensed [2]. The taxicabs must be owned by the temporary authorisation applicant [2]. The drivers of taxicabs must be employed by the temporary authorisation applicant and must be authorised to drive these taxicabs in the emirate in which the taxicabs are licensed [2]. An undertaking to keep taximeters running throughout trips must be submitted [2]. Drivers of Taxicabs or On-demand Taxicabs may not transport passengers without obtaining relevant permit from the RTA [3].
